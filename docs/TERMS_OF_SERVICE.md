# Terms of Service & Placement Code of Conduct

> **Notice**: This document outlines standard institutional terms of use and professional conduct rules for GSFC University placement platform users (Students, Corporate Recruiters, Faculty, and Alumni).

## 1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing the **GSFC University Placement Portal & AI Career Suite**, users agree to abide by these Terms of Service, university placement bylaws, and academic honesty codes.

## 2. Student Conduct & Offer Integrity

- **Truth in Reporting**: All information submitted in student profiles, resumes, CGPA declarations, and capstone project portfolios must be accurate and truthful.
- **Single-Offer Acceptance Policy (Dream Offer Rules)**: Once a candidate receives and formally accepts an official placement offer through the portal, subsequent eligibility rules defined by GSFC TPC (e.g. Dream / Core Tier upgrade thresholds) will govern future applications.
- **Anti-Cheating & Proctored Assessments**: Candidates must not use unauthorized multi-party assistance, automated screen capture bypasses, or proxy test-takers during online aptitude and technical coding assessments. Violations result in automatic session termination and disciplinary review.

## 3. Corporate Recruiter Obligations

- **Verification of Credentials**: Recruiters agree to use candidate resumes and contact details solely for evaluating employment eligibility for published recruitment drives.
- **Gated Publishing & Fair Evaluation**: Job drives must specify accurate CTC breakdowns, job locations, and minimum eligibility criteria without discriminatory screening.
- **Timely Feedback**: Recruiters are encouraged to update candidate pipeline stages (`shortlisted`, `interview`, `offered`, `rejected`) in a timely manner.

## 4. Institutional Disclaimers

GSFC University Training & Placement Cell provides AI matching scores, predictive placement analytics, and mock interview grades as advisory tools to assist student career readiness. Ultimate hiring determinations remain at the sole discretion of the participating employer.
