# Privacy Policy (Student PII & Institutional Data Governance)

> **Notice**: This document is an operational policy template designed for university campus placement systems handling student Personally Identifiable Information (PII). It is provided for technical compliance and must undergo institutional legal review prior to statutory deployment.

## 1. Information We Collect

GSFC University Placement Portal collects and processes information necessary to deliver campus recruitment and AI career coaching:
- **Academic & Profile Information**: Full name, student roll number, enrolled academic program (B.Tech, B.Sc, BBA, etc.), CGPA, and year of passing.
- **Resume & Project Dossiers**: Candidate-submitted PDF resumes, technical skill declarations, capstone project repositories, and past internship certifications.
- **AI Assessment Telemetry**: Speech pacing metrics (Words-Per-Minute), STAR methodology evaluation logs, and mock interview answers.
- **Cryptographic Placement Records**: Verifiable offer letters, digital credential hashes, and background verification (BGV) timestamps.

## 2. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected data is processed strictly for:
1. Matching candidates to eligible corporate recruitment drives and campus placement opportunities.
2. Generating AI resume match scores, interview prep scorecards, and predictive career recommendations.
3. Facilitating verified background checks by authorized hiring partners.
4. Compiling mandatory institutional accreditation reports (NAAC Metric 5.2.1, NIRF Table 3A, NBA Tier-1, AICTE Survey).

## 3. Data Protection & Security Controls

- **Encryption in Transit & at Rest**: All communications use TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords use bcrypt salt hashing.
- **Tamper-Evident Ledger**: Official placement offer letters and certificates are anchored in an immutable SHA-256 Merkle hash-chain ledger.
- **No Third-Party Commercial Data Brokerage**: Student PII and resumes are never sold or shared with unauthorized commercial data brokers.

## 4. Student Rights & Data Access

Students maintain the right to:
- Access their complete profile dossier and activity logs.
- Request correction of erroneous academic information through the TPC Administrator.
- Manage recruitment drive application submissions and privacy preferences.
